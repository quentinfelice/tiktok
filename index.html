studia

Illustrated slideshows, sent to your TikTok drafts.

Studia is a small creative tool. It renders illustrated photo slideshows, sends them to the connected TikTok account's drafts, and reads that account's post statistics to decide what to make next.

How it works

  1. You connect your TikTok account with "Log in with TikTok" and choose what Studia may access.
  2. Studia uploads each new slideshow to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is published by Studia.
  3. You open the draft in the TikTok app, pick a sound, review the caption and post it yourself.
  4. Studia reads the view, like, comment and share counts of your posts to improve the next slideshows.

You can disconnect Studia at any time from the app permissions in your TikTok settings.
