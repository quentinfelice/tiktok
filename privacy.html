Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

What Studia accesses

When you connect your TikTok account, Studia asks TikTok only for the permissions it needs:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): your TikTok user ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • Your posts (video.list): your recent posts and their public counts (views, likes, comments, shares), to learn which slideshows work.
  • Upload to drafts (video.upload): to send new slideshows to your TikTok inbox as drafts. Studia does not publish.

Studia does not access your messages, contacts, followers' personal data or payment information.

How it is used and stored

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored as encrypted secrets in the operator's private environment. They are never published or shared.
  • Post statistics are stored in the operator's private workspace, only to plan future slideshows.
  • This website has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.

Sharing

Studia does not sell, rent or share your data. Data goes only between Studia and TikTok's official API.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while you use Studia. Remove Studia's access at any time from the app permissions in your TikTok settings; this revokes its tokens. To have stored statistics deleted, open an issue.

Children

Studia is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the effective date above shows the latest version. Questions: open an issue.
