studia

Post an illustrated slideshow or video to TikTok

Studia sends illustrated photo slideshows and short animated videos to your own TikTok account. You review each post, choose who can see it, and confirm before anything is posted.

What Studia uses

  • Your profile name and avatar (user.info.basic), only to show which account is connected.
  • Photo and video posting, to send a slideshow or a video you selected to your account.
  • Your public posts and their counts (video.list), to show you how your slideshows perform.

Studia never posts on its own. You can disconnect it at any time from the app permissions in your TikTok settings.
